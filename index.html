I build the second source.

Most of these repositories decide something by producing an independent second artefact and comparing it against the first, rather than by asserting. A contract against the page as it is served. A ledger against a settlement report. The same agent with the policy on and off. It is the same move each time and it is deliberate, so it is better said here than discovered on the fourth repository and mistaken for a formula.

Underneath all of it is one idea: designing for the failure that does not announce itself. Systems that fail loudly are a solved problem. The expensive ones keep returning success.

15 of 16 carry the move. QUOTEZ does not, and saying so is the point: a thesis that survives only by leaving out the case that breaks it is not a thesis. That repository argues something narrower instead, that a capability which does not exist cannot be misused, and it has no second artefact because it has no write path at all.

Grouped by what they argue

Selection and measurement: a number is only as good as what it counts and what it was selected from

  • QUACKZ deflating a backtest that only looks good because it was picked out of two hundred
  • QUIZZ answering what a statistic said at the time, and refusing when it cannot
  • QUARRYZ keeping every version a statistical office published, and failing the build when it quietly issues another
  • QUASHZ refusing a row whose outcome had not been decided yet when the decision would have been made
  • QUALMZ a fixed number of looks at the holdout, where re-running the same configuration does not buy another

Designing for the failure that does not announce itself

  • QUEUEZ ordering a feed by its sequence, because on a real recorded session the clock goes backwards
  • QUANDARYZ counting the distinct screens a component can settle into when its responses arrive out of order
  • QUIETZ watching whether the data arrived rather than whether the server answered

A capability that does not exist cannot be misused

  • QUOTEZ market data an agent can read and cannot act on
  • QUELLZ measuring what prompt-injection containment costs in utility as well as in attack rate

A silent change in an external surface must become a loud stop

  • QUESTZ stopping a scraper before it writes a CSV from a page that changed shape
  • QUARANTINEZ treating an outcome the venue never confirmed as terminal rather than as a retry

A test is only worth what its boundary admits: an emulator result with no stated limit is a claim about production nobody made

  • QUILTZ proving infrastructure code wrong without a cloud account, and saying what that cannot show

Admission: who is calling, what may they reach, whose budget do they spend, and how much of the answer actually arrived

  • QUENCHZ deciding in the open what a tool server gets free while it is still somebody's subprocess

Noticing a bad deploy is not the hard part. Reaching for an instrument that cannot tell you which failure it is, and believing what it says, is the expensive one

  • QUAYZ telling a crash loop from an OOMKill, and naming the failure that no single field finds

Repetition and disorder in delivery must not change the arithmetic

  • QUIDZ refusing the outbound payment that would have gone out twice
